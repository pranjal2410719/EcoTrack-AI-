# EcoTrack AI — Future Plans

## Hackathon Differentiators (Shown as Roadmap)

### Carbon Receipt Scanner
- Scan electricity bills and fuel receipts
- AI extracts data automatically using OCR
- Eliminates manual data entry

### Green Route Planner
- Suggest walking, cycling, and public transport routes
- Prioritize lowest-carbon paths
- Integrate with Google Maps API

### Carbon Footprint Simulator
- "What if I stop using my car?"
- AI predicts annual impact of lifestyle changes
- Interactive scenario modeling

### Eco Shopping Advisor
- Scan product barcodes
- AI estimates product carbon footprint
- Suggest sustainable alternatives

### AI Sustainability Report
- Monthly PDF report with:
  - Footprint analysis
  - Progress tracking
  - Recommendations
  - Savings achieved

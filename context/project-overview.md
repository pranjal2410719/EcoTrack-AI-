# EcoTrack AI — Project Overview

## Project Name
EcoTrack AI

## Tagline
Your Personal Carbon Footprint Assistant — Track, Understand, and Reduce Your Environmental Impact.

## Purpose
EcoTrack AI helps individuals understand, track, and reduce their carbon footprint using simple lifestyle assessments and AI-powered personalized recommendations.

## Problem Statement
- Most people don't know their personal carbon footprint.
- Daily activities' environmental impact is not well understood.
- Lack of personalized guidance for reducing emissions.
- No easy way to measure the impact of eco-friendly actions.

## Solution
A web platform that calculates carbon footprint from a simple lifestyle questionnaire, provides AI-generated sustainability recommendations, and visualizes progress through an interactive dashboard.

## Target Audience
- Environmentally conscious individuals
- Students and young professionals
- Corporate employees (ESG initiatives)
- Educational institutions

## Business Model
- Freemium: Free basic tracking & insights
- Premium: Advanced analytics, AI coaching, smart integrations
- B2B: Schools, universities, corporates for ESG initiatives

## Key Differentiators
- AI-powered personalized recommendations via Gemini
- Simple onboarding (5-question assessment)
- Actionable weekly sustainability plans
- Progress tracking with historical comparison
